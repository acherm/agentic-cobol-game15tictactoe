  Notable observations:
  - Game of 6 with K=3 is degenerate: the only triple is {1,2,3} and no player
  can ever claim all three, so every game is a draw.
  - Game of 20 with K=9 is interesting: same number pool as Game of 15 but with
  only 4 triples (the "high end" triples), leading to many more draws (45% vs
  18%).

  rest draws.
